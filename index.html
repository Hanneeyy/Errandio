The problem

Student life moves fast. Errands don't fit in between.

  • ✕Back-to-back classes leave almost no time to grab food or run to the bookstore.
  • ✕Printing a reviewer or dropping off a project means choosing between the task and the next class.
  • ✕Existing delivery apps aren't built for campus distances, campus pricing, or campus trust.
The Errandio answer

Request, connect, and relax.

Post what you need, a verified student nearby accepts it, and you get updates while it's handled. One campus, one community, more things done.

How it works

From posted to done, four simple steps.

No app-hopping, no guesswork on price. Just a straightforward flow built around how a campus day actually works.

01

Post your errand

Describe the task, pick your zone, and see the price before you send it.

02

Student accepts

A verified ADDU student nearby claims it and gets moving.

03

Pay the price

One fixed, zone-based fee. No haggling, no surprise charges.

04

Errand done

Track progress in the app, then rate and tip if they went above and beyond.
